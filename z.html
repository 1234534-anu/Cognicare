How to play

1

Select a default image from the grid, or upload your own memory.

2

Choose how many pieces — 4, 6, or 9 — to challenge yourself.

3

Press Begin puzzle to scatter the pieces.

4

Drag pieces from the left panel and place them on the correct board slot.

5

Enable Ghost overlay for a transparent hint on the board.

How to Play N-Back

Step 1 — Watch the grid

A blue square appears on a 3×3 grid, one position at a time. Your only job is to remember where it appeared. Each square stays visible for 3 seconds — no rush.

Step 2 — Compare N steps back

In 2-Back, when you see Trial 3, compare it to Trial 1. In 3-Back, compare Trial 4 to Trial 1.

Example (2-Back): Trial 1 = Top-Left → Trial 3 = Top-Left → MATCH! Press "Match".

Step 3 — Press a button when you're ready

Press Match or No Match while the blue square is showing. If you're not sure, that's completely fine — the next square will appear automatically.

💙 There is no penalty for not answering. Just do your best!

Step 4 — Scoring and levels

Correct answer = points + streak bonus. Hit 5 correct in a row and level increases. Game lasts 2 minutes.

Correct = N × 10 points + streak bonus
5-streak = level up (harder!)
3 errors in a row = level down (easier)

Why this trains your brain

N-Back directly exercises working memory — the brain's short-term scratch pad. Research shows regular N-Back training can slow cognitive decline and improve attention, focus, and processing speed.

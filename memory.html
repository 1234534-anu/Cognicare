Guide

How to play.

1
Pick your level

Start from Level 1 or jump to any unlocked level from the Levels screen. Each level must be completed to unlock the next.

2
Flip two cards

Click any face-down card to reveal it. Then click a second card — if they match, they stay face-up and turn white.

3
Find all pairs

Clear the board by matching every pair. Fewer moves means a higher score. Each level has a different number of pairs.

4
Unlock the next

Complete a level to unlock the next one. Progress is saved automatically — pick up where you left off anytime.
